Come for the harbour. Stay for the food.

Right in the heart of Folkestone Harbour, The Captain's Table is the kind of place you can settle into. Start the morning with a proper breakfast, meet for brunch, grab a coffee and cake, or make lunch the reason to linger a little longer.

Everything is cooked to order and served with the friendly welcome our regulars keep coming back for.
